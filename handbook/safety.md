# Shop Safety

**Read this before using any tool.** A mentor must sign you off on each machine before you use
it alone.

## Always

- **Safety glasses on** whenever anyone is building, cutting, drilling, or testing the robot.
  In the shop and in the pit. No exceptions.
- **Closed-toe shoes.** Tie back long hair, no dangling jewelry or loose sleeves near machines.
- **Never work alone** with power tools.
- **Tell a mentor immediately** about any injury, even small ones.
- If you're unsure how to use something — **stop and ask.**

## Robot

- **Disable before you touch.** Announce "Disabled!" and have the Driver Station disabled
  before anyone reaches into the robot.
- Call out **"Enabling!"** loudly before enabling, and make sure everyone is clear.
- Treat mechanisms as loaded: springs, gas shocks, and elevators can move when power is off.
- **Main breaker off** before any electrical work.

## Batteries

- Inspect before use: no cracks, bulges, leaks, or loose terminals. Damaged = remove from service.
- Carry by the case/handle, never by the wires.
- Charge only on team chargers; never leave charging batteries unattended overnight.
- Acid spill → tell a mentor; use the battery spill kit (baking soda).

## Machines

| Tool | Key rules |
|---|---|
| CNC (X-Carve) | Stay with it while running; know where E-stop is; secure stock; hearing protection |
| Laser cutter | Never leave running; only approved materials (**no PVC/vinyl** — toxic gas); ventilation on |
| 3D printers | Hot end and bed are hot; don't reach in while printing |
| Drill press / bandsaw | Mentor sign-off required; clamp work; hands clear of blade path |
| Soldering | Use the stand; ventilation; wash hands after (lead) |
