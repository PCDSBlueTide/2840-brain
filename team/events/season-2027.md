## The game

- **Game:** BIOCORE™ presented by Haas
- **Season theme:** *FIRST*® CANOPY™ (2026–27, biodiversity-themed across all FIRST programs)
- **Teaser:** a new challenge where teams "delve into the heart of what sustains life on Earth".
  No rules or game pieces until kickoff.
- **Game reveal:** **Sat Jan 9, 2027, 10:00 AM Arizona time** (12:00 PM ET) on the
  [FRC YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@FIRSTRoboticsCompetition)

## Build timeline (fill in after kickoff)

Kickoff to AZ North is **12 weeks**. Week Zero (Feb 20) is a good target for a driving robot.

| Target | Goal |
|---|---|
| Jan 9 – 16 | Game analysis, strategy, robot archetype decided |
| Jan 17 – Jan 30 | Prototypes; drivetrain driving |
| Jan 31 – Feb 20 | Robot v1 built and wired; drive at Week Zero if we can |
| Feb 21 – Mar 30 | Iterate, software/auto, driver practice, spares, bumpers |

Track the actual work on the [2027 Robot board](https://github.com/orgs/PCDSBlueTide/projects/2)
(see [Planning board](../../handbook/planning-board.md)).
